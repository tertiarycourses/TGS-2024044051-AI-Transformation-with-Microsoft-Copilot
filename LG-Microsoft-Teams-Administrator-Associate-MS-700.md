## Course goal

This learner guide helps you practise the work of a Microsoft Teams administrator. You will plan and manage a Teams environment, configure security and governance, manage teams/channels/apps, administer meetings and calling, and troubleshoot Teams quality, clients, devices, sign-in, and AI experiences.

## Current Microsoft guide note

Microsoft Learn lists MS-700 skills measured as of **July 29, 2026**. The current objectives are: configure and manage a Teams environment, manage teams/channels/chats/apps, manage meetings and calling, and monitor/report/troubleshoot Teams.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the labs, you should be able to:

1. Plan Teams network bandwidth, ports, protocols, Network planner impact, and connectivity readiness tests.
2. Configure Teams security and compliance with Defender for Office 365, retention, sensitivity labels, DLP, Conditional Access, information barriers, communication compliance, and insider risk considerations.
3. Plan governance for Teams lifecycle, policy packages, group creation, group expiration, naming policies, access reviews, PowerShell, and Microsoft Graph operations.
4. Configure external access, guest access, SharePoint/OneDrive sharing, shared channels, B2B direct connect, cross-tenant access, and multitenant organization scenarios.
5. Manage Teams clients, Teams Rooms, devices, configuration profiles, firmware, tags, remote sign-in, resource accounts, and VDI requirements.
6. Create and manage teams, templates, membership, privacy, sensitivity, frontline experiences, channels, chats, messaging policies, private channels, and shared channels.
7. Manage Teams apps, app setup policies, app permissions, consent, blocked apps, extensibility options, app store settings, purchasing, and app uploads.
8. Configure meetings, appointments, webinars, town halls, meeting templates, meeting policies, event policies, customization policies, and Copilot settings.
9. Manage Teams Phone numbers, resource accounts, conferencing bridges, voice settings, voicemail, calling policies, auto attendants, and call queues.
10. Monitor usage, quality, alerts, guest access, feedback, network tests, client logs, cache, installation/update issues, sign-in problems, meeting join issues, and Copilot/AI experience issues.

## Case study used throughout the labs

You are the Teams administrator for **Contoso Hybrid Services**, an organization with 2,000 users, regional offices, frontline workers, guest collaborators, Teams Rooms, shared channels, webinars, town halls, and Teams Phone.

Contoso needs:

- Reliable voice, video, meeting, event, and calling quality.
- Secure and compliant collaboration.
- Controlled team lifecycle and naming.
- External collaboration with partners.
- Device and Teams Rooms management.
- Meeting and event governance.
- App governance and app deployment.
- Monitoring, reporting, and troubleshooting runbooks.

## How to complete each lab

1. Read the scenario and objective.
2. Use a Microsoft 365 tenant if available.
3. If no tenant is available, complete the policy and design worksheets from Microsoft Learn documentation.
4. Record screenshots, policy names, PowerShell snippets, and assumptions.
5. Answer checkpoint questions.
6. Save each deliverable for the final readiness review.

## Exam preparation guidance

For MS-700, focus on:

- Teams admin center locations and policy relationships.
- Microsoft 365 dependencies such as Entra ID, Purview, SharePoint, OneDrive, Defender, devices, licensing, and networking.
- Knowing when to use standard, private, and shared channels.
- Meeting, event, webinar, town hall, and Teams Phone policy behavior.
- Monitoring and troubleshooting signals.
- PowerShell and Microsoft Graph for operational tasks.
