# AI Transformation with Microsoft Copilot — Learner Guide
TGS-2024044051 · v2.0 · 27 September 2026

## Learning outcomes
- LO1: Develop technology implementation plans and business processes for Microsoft Copilot.
- LO2: Develop control procedures for Microsoft Copilot to manage security.
- LO3: Evaluate the integration of Microsoft Copilot to ensure alignment with business objectives.
- LO4: Develop optimization plans for Microsoft Copilot to improve business operations.

## Topic 1: Set the Copilot Transformation Strategy and Lead by Example

Official reference: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/ab-100/

### 01. Transformation ambition
- Input: A service division misses response targets
- Method: Use Copilot Chat to compare three improvement ambitions
- Output: One-page transformation mandate
- Control: CEO approves a measurable business problem
- Failure test: Ambition is only a technology slogan
- Measure: Target service-cycle reduction
- Practice: [Lab 01](labs/lab-01/README.md)

### 02. Value pool map
- Input: Five departments and task-volume estimates
- Method: Ask Copilot to cluster recurring knowledge-work opportunities
- Output: Value pool map with owner
- Control: Keep only opportunities tied to a named outcome
- Failure test: High-volume activity has no customer value
- Measure: Annual addressable hours by pool
- Practice: [Lab 01](labs/lab-01/README.md)

### 03. Executive sponsor
- Input: Four competing leadership priorities
- Method: Draft a sponsor brief in Word with Copilot
- Output: Sponsor decision and named accountable owner
- Control: Sponsor commits a review cadence
- Failure test: No executive owns the benefit
- Measure: Sponsor decisions closed on time
- Practice: [Lab 01](labs/lab-01/README.md)

### 04. Stakeholder alignment
- Input: Sales, service, finance and risk concerns
- Method: Summarise the competing positions with Copilot
- Output: Stakeholder trade-off matrix
- Control: Record dissent before selecting a pilot
- Failure test: Risk concern is omitted from the brief
- Measure: Unresolved stakeholder objections
- Practice: [Lab 01](labs/lab-01/README.md)

### 05. Success definition
- Input: Baseline cycle time and quality scores
- Method: Use Copilot to draft a result statement
- Output: Outcome scorecard with baseline and target
- Control: Board accepts denominator and time window
- Failure test: Activity count is presented as impact
- Measure: Target met on comparable work
- Practice: [Lab 01](labs/lab-01/README.md)

### 06. Copilot Chat briefing
- Input: Synthetic board pack and policy extract
- Method: Ask Copilot Chat for a sourced executive summary
- Output: Five-point decision brief
- Control: Verify each material claim against the pack
- Failure test: Plausible but unsupported claim
- Measure: Supported claims divided by claims
- Practice: [Lab 02](labs/lab-02/README.md)

### 07. Question refinement
- Input: A broad request for better service
- Method: Refine goal, context, constraints and format in Copilot
- Output: Reusable executive prompt
- Control: Prompt names audience and decision
- Failure test: Response is polished but irrelevant
- Measure: Decision-relevant points per answer
- Practice: [Lab 02](labs/lab-02/README.md)

### 08. Source challenge
- Input: Two contradictory policy versions
- Method: Ask Copilot to identify differences and uncertainty
- Output: Source conflict register
- Control: Owner resolves the authoritative version
- Failure test: Retired document becomes the basis
- Measure: Current-source citation rate
- Practice: [Lab 02](labs/lab-02/README.md)

### 09. Scenario comparison
- Input: Three service improvement options
- Method: Ask Copilot for assumptions and trade-offs
- Output: Option comparison table
- Control: Compare options with the same criteria
- Failure test: One option gets a favourable metric
- Measure: Criteria applied to all options
- Practice: [Lab 02](labs/lab-02/README.md)

### 10. Executive follow-up
- Input: A decision brief with open questions
- Method: Use Copilot to list evidence gaps and owners
- Output: Follow-up action register
- Control: No recommendation without missing facts
- Failure test: Open question disappears from summary
- Measure: Critical gaps assigned to owners
- Practice: [Lab 02](labs/lab-02/README.md)

### 11. Word strategy memo
- Input: A synthetic service improvement brief
- Method: Draft a decision memo with Copilot in Word
- Output: Two-page strategy memo
- Control: Human executive signs the recommendation
- Failure test: Draft invents a savings figure
- Measure: Verified material claims
- Practice: [Lab 03](labs/lab-03/README.md)

### 12. Recommendation logic
- Input: Three options and a cost ceiling
- Method: Ask Copilot to test the logic of a recommendation
- Output: Recommendation with assumptions
- Control: State rejected alternatives and rationale
- Failure test: Trade-off is hidden
- Measure: Board questions answered with evidence
- Practice: [Lab 03](labs/lab-03/README.md)

### 13. PowerPoint board story
- Input: Approved strategy memo and metrics
- Method: Create a concise executive deck with Copilot in PowerPoint
- Output: Five-slide board narrative
- Control: Every chart traces to approved numbers
- Failure test: Slide claims exceed the source
- Measure: Figures verified before review
- Practice: [Lab 03](labs/lab-03/README.md)

### 14. Decision appendix
- Input: Risk and sensitivity notes
- Method: Use Copilot to structure board Q&A
- Output: Board appendix and question log
- Control: Distinguish evidence from assumptions
- Failure test: Appendix repeats unsupported claims
- Measure: Questions with grounded answers
- Practice: [Lab 03](labs/lab-03/README.md)

### 15. Leadership communication
- Input: Approved board decision
- Method: Draft a leader message with Copilot in Outlook
- Output: Change announcement for review
- Control: No send until approved by sponsor
- Failure test: Message promises unapproved outcomes
- Measure: Message comprehension in pilot survey
- Practice: [Lab 03](labs/lab-03/README.md)

## Topic 2: Govern Copilot Use and Protect Business Decisions

Official reference: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/copilot-controls/security-governance

### 16. Information boundary
- Input: Synthetic confidential account file
- Method: Decide which content may be used with Copilot
- Output: Data-use decision table
- Control: Data owner approves permitted sources
- Failure test: Sensitive source is broadly shared
- Measure: Restricted exposures found in review
- Practice: [Lab 04](labs/lab-04/README.md)

### 17. Permission reality
- Input: Two roles with different access
- Method: Request the same summary under each approved identity
- Output: Access-test evidence record
- Control: Security owner reviews unexpected access
- Failure test: User receives material outside role
- Measure: Unexpected accessible files
- Practice: [Lab 04](labs/lab-04/README.md)

### 18. Source reliability
- Input: Current and retired policies
- Method: Compare Copilot answer with authoritative source
- Output: Claim-verification worksheet
- Control: Reviewer checks every material statement
- Failure test: Retired policy cited as current
- Measure: Supported claims divided by claims
- Practice: [Lab 04](labs/lab-04/README.md)

### 19. Human approval
- Input: Draft external customer commitment
- Method: Assign a human approval point before release
- Output: Approval decision record
- Control: Named owner signs external commitment
- Failure test: Copilot text is sent without review
- Measure: Unapproved external actions
- Practice: [Lab 04](labs/lab-04/README.md)

### 20. Responsible-use rule
- Input: A sensitive executive scenario
- Method: Draft a plain-language use rule in Word
- Output: One-page responsible-use charter
- Control: Legal and risk owners approve wording
- Failure test: Charter is too vague to apply
- Measure: Exceptions resolved within SLA
- Practice: [Lab 04](labs/lab-04/README.md)

### 21. Risk appetite
- Input: Five use cases with different consequences
- Method: Rank harms, controls and owners with Copilot
- Output: Risk-tier matrix
- Control: High-impact uses require stronger review
- Failure test: All use cases share one weak gate
- Measure: High-risk use cases with owner
- Practice: [Lab 05](labs/lab-05/README.md)

### 22. Incident response
- Input: Synthetic disclosure near miss
- Method: Ask Copilot to organise facts and response options
- Output: Incident decision timeline
- Control: Security owner determines escalation
- Failure test: Evidence is lost or blame is assigned early
- Measure: Time to containment
- Practice: [Lab 05](labs/lab-05/README.md)

### 23. Quality challenge
- Input: Confident answer with two errors
- Method: Use Copilot as a critic, then verify manually
- Output: Red-team finding and correction
- Control: Release only after material errors corrected
- Failure test: Fluent answer is treated as proof
- Measure: Critical errors per review set
- Practice: [Lab 05](labs/lab-05/README.md)

### 24. Policy ownership
- Input: Business, IT, legal and HR responsibilities
- Method: Draft a RACI with Copilot
- Output: Governance RACI
- Control: No control lacks a decision owner
- Failure test: Everyone assumes IT owns content quality
- Measure: Controls with named owners
- Practice: [Lab 05](labs/lab-05/README.md)

### 25. Board assurance
- Input: Pilot results and unresolved risks
- Method: Summarise assurance evidence with Copilot
- Output: Board risk-and-control dashboard
- Control: Disclose unresolved material risks
- Failure test: Green status masks a severe issue
- Measure: Critical issues overdue
- Practice: [Lab 05](labs/lab-05/README.md)

## Topic 4: Measure Value, Scale Adoption and Report to the Board

Official reference: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/copilot-control-system/measurement-reporting

### 36. Use-case portfolio
- Input: Twelve candidate workflows
- Method: Prioritise volume, value, feasibility and risk
- Output: Ranked opportunity portfolio
- Control: Reject ownerless or unsafe use case
- Failure test: Highest score hides weak data
- Measure: Validated value per use case
- Practice: [Lab 08](labs/lab-08/README.md)

### 37. Adoption baseline
- Input: Usage by role and department
- Method: Compare active use with licensed seats
- Output: Adoption baseline chart
- Control: Separate sign-in from productive use
- Failure test: Licence assignment counted as adoption
- Measure: Weekly active users per cohort
- Practice: [Lab 08](labs/lab-08/README.md)

### 38. Enablement design
- Input: Three role-specific skill gaps
- Method: Ask Copilot to draft role-based coaching plans
- Output: 90-day enablement calendar
- Control: Manager commits time for practice
- Failure test: One generic training suits no one
- Measure: Practice completion by role
- Practice: [Lab 08](labs/lab-08/README.md)

### 39. Feedback loop
- Input: Pilot questions and complaints
- Method: Cluster feedback themes with Copilot
- Output: Prioritised improvement backlog
- Control: Assign owner and due date
- Failure test: Prompt advice hides a source defect
- Measure: Median defect closure time
- Practice: [Lab 08](labs/lab-08/README.md)

### 40. Scale gate
- Input: Pilot adoption, quality and risk data
- Method: Prepare go, hold or stop recommendation
- Output: Wave-two decision paper
- Control: Every critical threshold passes
- Failure test: Average score hides a severe failure
- Measure: Criteria passed divided by required
- Practice: [Lab 08](labs/lab-08/README.md)

### 41. Excel value model
- Input: Synthetic task time and cost worksheet
- Method: Use Copilot in Excel to analyse observed changes
- Output: Net time-saved table
- Control: Review time is deducted from gross saving
- Failure test: Gross saving is sold as net value
- Measure: Net minutes saved per task
- Practice: [Lab 09](labs/lab-09/README.md)

### 42. Benefit confidence
- Input: Observed sample and management estimates
- Method: Ask Copilot to label evidence strength
- Output: Benefit confidence register
- Control: Assumption is never described as measured
- Failure test: Weak sample is extrapolated widely
- Measure: Measured share of claimed value
- Practice: [Lab 09](labs/lab-09/README.md)

### 43. Cost of ownership
- Input: Seats, enablement and support costs
- Method: Use Copilot to compare total cost scenarios
- Output: Total-cost table
- Control: Include recurring change and review effort
- Failure test: License fee is the only cost
- Measure: Cost per realised outcome
- Practice: [Lab 09](labs/lab-09/README.md)

### 44. Sensitivity test
- Input: Low, base and high adoption scenarios
- Method: Ask Copilot to vary key assumptions
- Output: Sensitivity chart with break-even point
- Control: Decision holds under a credible downside
- Failure test: Single optimistic case drives approval
- Measure: Break-even adoption threshold
- Practice: [Lab 09](labs/lab-09/README.md)

### 45. Investment decision
- Input: Value model and risk register
- Method: Draft a balanced investment memo in Word
- Output: Fund, hold or stop recommendation
- Control: Sponsor signs conditions and budget
- Failure test: Recommendation ignores risk-adjusted cost
- Measure: Conditions met before release
- Practice: [Lab 09](labs/lab-09/README.md)

### 46. Quality scorecard
- Input: Sample outputs and evidence checks
- Method: Summarise quality results with Copilot
- Output: Quality trend chart
- Control: Any critical safety failure blocks scale
- Failure test: Good average masks one severe error
- Measure: Critical failures per sample
- Practice: [Lab 10](labs/lab-10/README.md)

### 47. Customer outcome
- Input: Service response and satisfaction data
- Method: Compare customer metrics before and after pilot
- Output: Customer-impact summary
- Control: Control for work mix and seasonality
- Failure test: AI usage is mistaken for customer benefit
- Measure: Comparable cycle-time change
- Practice: [Lab 10](labs/lab-10/README.md)

### 48. Board reporting
- Input: Adoption, value, quality and risk metrics
- Method: Use Copilot in PowerPoint to draft a board update
- Output: One-page board scorecard
- Control: Every number has source and date
- Failure test: Board pack hides uncertainty
- Measure: Claims with traceable evidence
- Practice: [Lab 10](labs/lab-10/README.md)

### 49. Next-quarter roadmap
- Input: Pilot findings and capacity limits
- Method: Ask Copilot to sequence the next three waves
- Output: 90-day roadmap with gates
- Control: Each wave has owner and stop rule
- Failure test: Scale outruns governance capacity
- Measure: Wave gates met on schedule

### 50. Executive decision rehearsal
- Input: Board challenge questions
- Method: Use Copilot to surface counterarguments
- Output: Final go, hold or stop record
- Control: Executive states assumptions and dissent
- Failure test: Decision is made on a polished demo
- Measure: Decision quality rubric score
- Practice: [Lab 10](labs/lab-10/README.md)
