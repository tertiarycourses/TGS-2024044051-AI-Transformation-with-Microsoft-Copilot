# Lab 7 - Teams Apps, App Setup Policies, Permissions, Consent, Extensibility, Store, and Uploads

## Objective

Manage Teams apps, org-wide app settings, setup policies, app permissions, consent, blocked apps, app store purchasing, store customization, extensibility options, and app uploads.

## Scenario

Contoso wants standard apps for all users, specialized apps for departments, and controlled third-party app usage.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Inventory app needs

Create:

| User group | Required app | Deployment method |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|

### Step 2 - Plan app policies

Create:

| Policy | Users | Apps pinned or blocked |
|--------|-------|------------------------|

### Step 3 - Review permissions and consent

Document:

- App permission policy
- User consent risk
- Admin approval process
- Blocked app process

### Step 4 - Recommend extensibility options

Compare apps, tabs, meeting apps, messaging extensions, and workflows.

### Step 5 - Plan app store management

Include purchasing, store appearance customization, and app upload process.

## Deliverable

Submit app inventory, app policy table, permissions/consent plan, extensibility comparison, and app store management plan.

## Checkpoint questions

1. What is the difference between app setup and app permission policies?
2. Why does app consent matter?
3. When should an app be uploaded directly to Teams?
