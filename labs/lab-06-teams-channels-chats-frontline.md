# Lab 6 - Teams Rollout, Templates, Membership, Privacy, Sensitivity, Frontline Teams, Channels, and Chats

## Objective

Create and manage teams, templates, membership, privacy, sensitivity, frontline teams, channels, chats, and messaging policies.

## Scenario

Contoso needs a structured Teams rollout for departments, projects, frontline staff, and partner workspaces.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Plan rollout

Create:

| Group | Rollout method | Training need |
|-------|----------------|---------------|

### Step 2 - Choose team creation method

Compare Teams admin center, Teams client, PowerShell, Graph, existing Microsoft 365 group, SharePoint site, and template.

### Step 3 - Design channels

Create:

| Channel | Type | Membership | Reason |
|---------|------|------------|--------|

Include standard, private, and shared channels.

### Step 4 - Configure policies

Plan messaging policies, channel creation policies, and sharing policies.

### Step 5 - Plan frontline teams

Document frontline team structure, membership, policy needs, and lifecycle.

## Deliverable

Submit rollout plan, creation method comparison, channel design, policy plan, and frontline team plan.
