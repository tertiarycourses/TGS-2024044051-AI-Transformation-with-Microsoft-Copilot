# Lab 4 - External Collaboration, Guest Access, Shared Channels, Cross-Tenant Access, and MTO

## Objective

Configure and manage external collaboration in Teams, including external access, guest access, shared channels, cross-tenant access, and multitenant organization scenarios.

## Scenario

Contoso collaborates with suppliers, training partners, and project vendors.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Compare collaboration methods

Create:

| Method | Best for | Risk |
|--------|----------|------|

Include external access, guest access, shared channels, and MTO.

### Step 2 - Plan tenant-level settings

Document:

- Teams external access
- Teams guest access
- Entra external collaboration
- SharePoint and OneDrive sharing

### Step 3 - Design shared channel access

Create:

| Partner | Cross-tenant setting | Channel purpose |
|---------|---------------------|-----------------|

### Step 4 - Control guest lifecycle

Define:

- Sponsor
- Expiration
- Access review
- Removal process
- Team-specific controls

### Step 5 - Create risk controls

List DLP, sensitivity labels, Conditional Access, and information barriers as appropriate.

## Deliverable

Submit collaboration comparison, tenant settings plan, shared channel design, guest lifecycle plan, and risk controls.

1. What is the difference between guest access and external access?
