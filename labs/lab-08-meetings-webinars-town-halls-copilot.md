# Lab 8 - Meetings, Webinars, Town Halls, Templates, Copilot Settings, and Event Policies

## Objective

Manage Teams meetings, appointments, webinars, town halls, meeting templates, meeting policies, event policies, customization policies, and Microsoft 365 Copilot settings.

## Scenario

Contoso runs internal meetings, customer webinars, executive town halls, and appointment-based sessions.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Recommend meeting type

Create:

| Requirement | Recommended type | Reason |
|-------------|------------------|--------|

Include meetings, webinars, town halls, and appointments.

### Step 2 - Plan meeting policies

Document:

- Recording
- Transcription
- Lobby
- Chat
- Content sharing
- Copilot setting

### Step 3 - Design templates

Create meeting templates for executive meeting, external webinar, and confidential project meeting.

### Step 4 - Plan event policies

Document webinar and town hall policy settings.

### Step 5 - Create customization plan

Plan branding, backgrounds, and meeting customization policies.

## Deliverable

Submit meeting type recommendation, meeting policy plan, template designs, event policy plan, and customization plan.

## Checkpoint questions

1. When should a webinar be used instead of a meeting?
2. Why do Copilot settings matter for meetings?
3. What policy affects who can record meetings?
