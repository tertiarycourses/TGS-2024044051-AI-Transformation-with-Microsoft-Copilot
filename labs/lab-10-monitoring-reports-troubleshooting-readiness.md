# Lab 10 - Monitoring, Reports, Alerts, Client Logs, Cache, Sign-In, Meeting Join, AI Experience Troubleshooting, and Readiness

## Objective

Monitor, report, troubleshoot Teams, and complete an MS-700 readiness review.

## Scenario

Contoso users report meeting join failures, poor audio quality, sign-in issues, and occasional Copilot experience problems.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Define reports

Create:

| Report | Audience | Decision supported |
|--------|----------|--------------------|

Include usage, team activity, app usage, active users, meeting metrics, storage, guest access, and creation/deletion reports.

### Step 2 - Configure alert plan

Document alert rules for service health, quality, device issues, and security/compliance events.

### Step 3 - Troubleshoot client issues

Create checklist:

- Collect client-side logs
- Clear Teams cache
- Check installation and update status
- Review client health
- Test sign-in
- Use self-help diagnostics

### Step 4 - Troubleshoot meeting and AI issues

Document checks for joining meetings, accessing meeting features, and Microsoft 365 Copilot/AI experiences in Teams.

### Step 5 - Build readiness tracker

Create:

| MS-700 area | Confidence 1-5 | Evidence from labs | Next study action |
|-------------|----------------|--------------------|-------------------|

Use the four current MS-700 skill areas.

## Deliverable

Submit report matrix, alert plan, client troubleshooting checklist, meeting/AI troubleshooting notes, and readiness tracker.

## Checkpoint questions

1. Which report helps identify adoption issues?
2. When should client cache be cleared?
3. Which MS-700 domain needs your most review?
