# Lab 3 - Governance, Policy Packages, Group Lifecycle, Access Reviews, PowerShell, and Graph

## Objective

Plan Teams governance, policy packages, policy assignment, Microsoft 365 group lifecycle, access reviews, and Teams operations with PowerShell and Microsoft Graph.

## Scenario

Contoso has unmanaged team creation and inconsistent policies for departments.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Define governance rules

Create:

| Governance area | Rule | Owner |
|-----------------|------|-------|

Include team creation, naming, expiration, archive, delete, restore, and access reviews.

### Step 2 - Plan policy packages

Create:

| User group | Policy package | Reason |
|------------|----------------|--------|

### Step 3 - Plan group lifecycle

Document:

- Microsoft 365 group creation settings
- Naming policy
- Blocked words
- Expiration policy
- Archive and restore process

### Step 4 - Write PowerShell examples

Document commands for connecting to Teams PowerShell and reviewing teams or policies.

### Step 5 - Identify Graph operations

List where Microsoft Graph can help with team creation, membership, and reporting.

## Deliverable

Submit governance rules, policy package plan, group lifecycle plan, PowerShell snippets, and Graph operation notes.

## Checkpoint questions

1. Why is naming policy useful?
2. When should a team be archived instead of deleted?
3. Why use PowerShell or Graph for Teams operations?
