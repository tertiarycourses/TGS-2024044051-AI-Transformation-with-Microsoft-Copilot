# Lab 9 - Teams Phone, Phone Numbers, Resource Accounts, Conferencing, Voicemail, Auto Attendants, and Call Queues

## Objective

Manage Teams Phone numbers, user and service numbers, resource accounts, conferencing bridges, voice settings, voicemail policies, auto attendants, call queues, and calling policies.

## Scenario

Contoso needs Teams Phone for support agents, reception, and regional managers.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Plan phone number inventory

Create:

| Number type | Assigned to | Purpose |
|-------------|-------------|---------|

Include user, service, and conferencing bridge numbers.

### Step 2 - Plan licensing and accounts

Document Teams Phone license needs, resource accounts, and service account ownership.

### Step 3 - Configure voice policy plan

Create:

| Policy | Users | Key setting |
|--------|-------|-------------|

Include calling policy, voicemail policy, and voice settings.

### Step 4 - Design auto attendant

Plan:

- Greeting
- Business hours
- Menu options
- Operator
- After-hours routing

### Step 5 - Design call queue

Plan agents, routing method, timeout, overflow, music on hold, and reporting.

## Deliverable

Submit phone number inventory, licensing/account plan, voice policy plan, auto attendant design, and call queue design.

## Checkpoint questions

1. What is a resource account used for?
2. When is a call queue appropriate?
3. What is the difference between user and service numbers?
