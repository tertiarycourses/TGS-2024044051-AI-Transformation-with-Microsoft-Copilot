# Lab 5 - Teams Clients, Devices, Rooms, Tags, Remote Sign-In, Firmware, and VDI

## Objective

Manage Teams clients, Teams devices, configuration profiles, Teams Rooms, device tags, remote sign-in, firmware, resource accounts, and VDI.

## Scenario

Contoso has meeting rooms, Teams displays, phones, and VDI users.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Inventory devices

Create:

| Device type | Location | Owner | Required license |
|-------------|----------|-------|------------------|

### Step 2 - Plan Teams Rooms accounts

Document:

- Resource account
- License
- Calendar processing
- Room mailbox
- Device assignment

### Step 3 - Configure device management plan

Include configuration profiles, firmware updates, device settings, and tags.

### Step 4 - Plan remote sign-in

Describe steps and security considerations for provisioning new devices.

### Step 5 - Plan Teams for VDI

Document requirements, limitations, user groups, and support process.

## Deliverable

Submit device inventory, Teams Rooms account plan, device management plan, remote sign-in steps, and VDI plan.

## Checkpoint questions

1. Why do Teams Rooms need resource accounts?
2. What is the benefit of device tags?
3. What should be checked before deploying Teams on VDI?
